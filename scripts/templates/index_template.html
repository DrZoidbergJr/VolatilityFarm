06 Delta

The Delta tracker below shows possible relationships between each ticker and its related instruments. The relation part is measured based on persistent statistical relations between each ticker and a basket of corresponding peers, upwards/downwards supply chain companies.

Each relationship is measured in four ways. A relative relation compares percentage moves (% to %), while an absolute relation compares dollar moves (dollar for dollar). Each of these is calculated twice: once adjusted for fundamental differences between the instruments and once without that adjustment.

Strength counts how many of those four versions show a strong co-dependency, so it ranges up to 4. A strength of 4 means all four versions agree.

Tickers are sorted by strength first, highest at the top. Tickers with the same strength are then sorted by the largest delta, meaning the largest spread available to earn.

Delta is the current spread. The dashed lines show how that spread is expected to move over the next 30 days. These estimates are updated daily and may shift within their error bounds, because they're uncertain.

The second row shows the position weights needed to trade that delta. For example, in a relative relation, holding 1% exposure in the main ticker requires the exposures shown below in each of the other tickers.

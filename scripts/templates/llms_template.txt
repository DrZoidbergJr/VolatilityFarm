# Volatility Farm

> Independent short-term research on stocks in the days after they report earnings - what changed, what the market got right or wrong, and the risks over the next 10-30 days.

Volatility Farm publishes one research report per company shortly after its earnings release, plus site-wide sections tracking an earnings calendar, cross-asset market regimes, and the realised performance of the ideas published here. Reports are informational research, not investment advice.

## Site sections

- [Calendar](https://volatilityfarm.com/#calendar): Upcoming and recent earnings dates for tracked tickers, with expected and realised price-impact statistics around the print.
- [Market Regimes](https://volatilityfarm.com/#regimes): Persistency (mean-reverting vs trending) and volatility (calm vs volatile) regime scatter plots for SP500, inverse volatility, gold, and short/intermediate/long-term bond proxies, traced over the last 30 days.
- [Performance](https://volatilityfarm.com/#performance): Realised performance of the portfolio implied by the published reports, benchmarked against SPX and VTI, with summary metrics (beta, alpha, Sharpe, Calmar, max drawdown, average position duration).
- [Reports archive](https://volatilityfarm.com/#reports): Full chronological list of every published company report.
- [Method](https://volatilityfarm.com/#method): How reports are built - scope-before-data, sourced tables, no investment advice, in-house analytics.
- [About](https://volatilityfarm.com/#about): Who publishes this, coverage, community, and contact.

## Reports

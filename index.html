06 How the reports are made
One question
Scope before data

Each report answers a single question stated on the first page. If the data can't answer it, the report says so and stops.

Sources shown
Tables you can check

Every chart has the table behind it. Sample sizes, windows and exclusions sit in the colophon, not in a footnote you have to email for.

No book
Nothing to sell you

No positions, no managed money, no affiliate links. The verdict at the end of each report is a judgment, not a recommendation.

Informational research
Not investment advice

Information is aggregated as is and does not constitute a financial investment suggestion. Informational purposes only.

Quantitative measures
Our own analytics

The analytics and market measures in each report are built in-house. Their inputs and outputs are shown; the methodology behind them stays proprietary.

07 About us
A small desk that publishes what it finds, whether or not it's interesting.

Volatility Farm is an independent research desk. We pick what might be interesting for us in the given week, gather the data ourselves, and publish the working alongside the conclusion. Reports that fail to find anything get published too, because knowing a signal isn't there is worth as much as knowing it is.

We derive our own investment decisions based on these reports, manage no outside money, and take no sponsorship.
